Manage VEX native and EVM accounts, send and receive assets, and connect to DApps with Wisp. Review connections and transactions in your wallet before approving them.

## Built around Vexanium

- **VEX native + EVM accounts.** Create or import accounts and switch between the networks you use.
- **Send and receive.** Transfer assets, share your receiving address, and follow transaction activity.
- **Connect to DApps.** Choose which sites can connect and review each signing request in your wallet.
- **Manage VEX resources.** Stake CPU/NET, use PowerUp, and manage RAM and REX.
- **Light and dark.** Choose the appearance you prefer on Android or in Chrome.

## Get started

### Android

1. Open the [latest Android release](https://github.com/windvex/wisp-wallet-releases/releases/latest) and download the `.apk` file.
2. Open the file on your phone and follow the installation prompts.
3. Open Wisp to create or import your accounts.

To update, install the newer APK over your existing installation.

### Chrome

1. Open the [Chrome release](https://github.com/windvex/wisp-wallet-releases/releases/tag/wisp-extension-v0.2.5) and download the file named `wisp-wallet-extension-*.zip`.
2. Extract it to a permanent folder.
3. Open `chrome://extensions` and enable **Developer mode**.
4. Select **Load unpacked**, choose the extracted folder, and pin Wisp to your toolbar.

To update, replace the files in the same folder and click **Reload** on Wisp. Keep the existing installation folder so Chrome can continue using its wallet data.

## Keep your accounts safe

Keep a backup for every account you want to recover. Never share your recovery phrase, private key, or wallet password in a support message.

Download Wisp from this repository's [official releases](https://github.com/windvex/wisp-wallet-releases/releases). Download checksums are included with the release files.
